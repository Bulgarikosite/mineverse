World Biomes

From towering jungle canopies to the scorching Nether, every biome holds unique treasures and challenges.

🌲

Dark Oak Forest

Dense canopies block sunlight, making hostile mob spawns more frequent even during the day. Rich in wood and mushrooms.

Wood-Rich Dangerous Mushrooms
🏜️

Desert Temple

Sandstone pyramids hide treasure chests — but also pressure-plate TNT traps. Loot includes golden apples and enchanted books.

Treasure Traps Hot & Dry
🌊

Deep Ocean

Guardians guard ocean monuments filled with prismarine and sea lanterns. Gold blocks lie at the core if you dare dive deep.

Prismarine Gold Guardians
🔥

Nether Wastes

A hellish dimension of lava and hostile Piglins. Essential for brewing potions and finding Blaze rods to reach The End.

Extreme Danger Blaze Rods
❄️

Ice Spikes

A rare biome with towering packed ice pillars. Polar bears and rabbits roam while resources remain scarce but beautiful.

Packed Ice Rare Cold
🦜

Jungle Temple

Hidden temples contain levers, tripwires, and dart traps. Solve the puzzle to claim emeralds, bones, and iron ingots.

Lush Puzzles Parrots
