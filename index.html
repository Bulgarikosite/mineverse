What is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a sandbox game where you can build literally anything out of blocks. You gather resources, craft tools, fight monsters, and explore an infinite randomly generated world.

It was made by Markus "Notch" Persson in 2009 and bought by Microsoft in 2014 for $2.5 billion. It has since become the best-selling video game of all time with over 300 million copies sold.

There's no real goal — you can beat the Ender Dragon to "win", but most people just keep building and exploring forever.

Game Modes

Pick how you want to play — there's a mode for everyone.

Survival

The classic mode. You have health and hunger, mobs attack at night, and you have to gather everything from scratch. This is how most people play.

Creative

You get every block for free, you can fly, and nothing can kill you. Perfect for building big projects or just messing around.

Hardcore

Same as Survival but if you die, your world is gone forever. Literally the hardest way to play. One mistake = start over.

Spectator

You can fly through walls and watch the world but can't interact with anything. Great for exploring or watching other players.

Pro Tips

Always get wood first

You can't do anything without wood. Punch trees as soon as you spawn, make planks, then a crafting table.

Sleep every night

Sleeping in a bed skips the night AND sets your spawn point. Die without a bed and you respawn at world spawn.

Light up your base

Mobs only spawn in darkness. Put torches everywhere — walls, ceilings, and outside. No torches = zombie invasion.

Never mine straight down

You could fall into lava or a cave. Always mine at an angle or use staircases to go underground.

Get diamonds ASAP

Diamonds are at Y level -58 or lower. Strip mine at that level or find an ancient city. Worth it — best gear in base game.

History

From a solo dev project to the best-selling game ever.

09

2009 — The Beginning

Markus "Notch" Persson released the first version of Minecraft (called "Cave Game") on May 17, 2009. It was super basic but people immediately loved it.

11

2011 — Official Release

Minecraft officially launched on November 18, 2011 at Minecon in Las Vegas. By then it already had 4 million players. Notch announced it live on stage.

12

2012 — Goes Everywhere

Minecraft came out on Xbox 360 and sold 400,000 copies in the first 24 hours. Then came iOS, Android, and PS3 versions. It was everywhere.

14

2014 — Microsoft Buys It

Microsoft bought Minecraft and Mojang for $2.5 billion. Notch left the company after the sale. Jens "Jeb" Bergensten took over as lead developer.

19

2019 — 10 Year Anniversary

Minecraft turned 10 and broke records. Hit 112 million monthly active players. Became the #1 most watched game on YouTube. Also introduced the buzzy bees update.

25

2025 — Still Going

Minecraft is still getting updates and is still the best-selling game ever with over 300 million copies sold. The movie came out in 2025 too and made over a billion dollars.
