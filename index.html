Biomes

Different areas of the world with their own vibes and resources.

Dark Oak Forest

Super dense trees block sunlight so mobs can spawn even in daytime. Watch out!

Desert Temple

Has treasure chests BUT also TNT traps underneath. Check before you dig!

Deep Ocean

Ocean monuments have gold blocks in the middle, but Guardians protect them.

The Nether

Basically hell. Lava everywhere. You need to go here to beat the game though.

Ice Spikes

Really rare biome with massive ice towers. Looks insane but hard to build in.

Jungle

Giant trees and hidden temples with puzzles. Also the only place to find parrots!
